    How to play

    Monsters walk S → 1 → 2 → 3 → E by the shortest route. Build a maze to make that route as long as possible. You can't fully block it.

    Rocklets are free maze blocks: drag to paint them, then click one with a creature selected to swap it. Every 10th wave is a boss.

    Knights have armor that takes a flat amount off every hit, so big hits and poison work best. Medics travel with Grunts and heal them, so kill the Medics first. Slimes split in two when killed.

    Each creature deals its element's damage. Enemies take double damage from elements they're weak to and half (or none) from ones they resist: check the upcoming waves. Creatures evolve at levels 5 and 10.

    Diggers can't be slowed or stunned, and as Ground types they ignore Electric completely. Rumblets stomp the ground and hit every walking enemy in range at once.

    The Daily challenge is one map a day, the same for everyone, with its own leaderboard tab. Achievements are in the menu (⚙).

    Cloudlets don't attack: they boost the creatures around them (a purple dot marks a boosted creature). Several don't stack.

    Tap or click a creature to upgrade it. There's no level cap. Selling always refunds everything you spent on it.

    The game saves automatically between waves. If you close it mid-wave, you restart that wave.
